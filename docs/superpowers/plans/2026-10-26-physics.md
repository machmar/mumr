# Physics Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A shared rigid-body physics module for MUMR (`engine/client/physics.js`) and the cube moved onto it, so the cube falls, bounces, tumbles, slides, is held at an angle and can be thrown like a hard plastic block.

**Architecture:** One UMD file, no THREE, no DOM (same style as `engine/client/room.js`), namespace `M.physics`. Boxes with a quaternion orientation and angular velocity, fixed 1/240 s substeps, corner-against-surface contacts from the room's levels/blocks/walls, sequential impulses with restitution and Coulomb friction, hands as kinematic spheres and plates. The holder of a body's token simulates it; others draw its packets.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JS (UMD, no build), `node --test` (Node 24), the existing engine (`engine/client/room.js`, `grab.js`, `body.js`, `wire.js`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-physics-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Plain JS files in the UMD style of the other engine files, no build step, no new dependency, no THREE or DOM in `physics.js` and `cube.js`. (Task 7 added the one dependency, `jolt-physics`, for the PC host only.)
- Floor plan: x, y on the floor, z up; THREE sees (x, y, z) as (x, z, -y).
- g = 9.81 m/s²; substep 1/240 s; a body moves at most 2 cm per substep (subdivide when faster).
- Defaults: density 1050 kg/m³, restitution 0.45, friction 0.45 static and 0.4 kinetic; cube size 0.12 m.
- Hand reach (fingertip/joint contact margin) 0.012 m; hand speed clamp 4 m/s; hold spring 20, hold speed 5 m/s, break-away 0.3 m; pinch grab 3 cm, fist grab 8 cm.
- Tests pass from the repo root in PowerShell with `node --test` (108 today) and the Python tests untouched.
- `apps/lab/boxes.js` is not changed. The packet for the cube grows from 32 to 56 bytes (14 float32).

## Review Focus

- A frame hitch (dt of 0.2 s) must not tunnel or explode: dt is clamped to 0.05 s and substepped.
- No room (`room` is null): the floor at z = 0 only, as the cube does today.
- A body put inside a wall, a table or a solid object (`put`) is pushed out, never stuck.
- A body thrown up under a table or into the ceiling bounces off the underside.
- A body resting exactly on a table edge tips off, does not jitter.
- A hold pulled through a wall lets go after 0.3 m; a hand that teleports (velocity above the clamp) does not launch the cube.
- NaN or infinite input (hands, packets) is ignored, never stored in a body.
- The room replaced while bodies rest on it wakes them.

### Task 4: Hands, hold, throw, flat-hand plate

**Files:**
- Modify: `engine/client/physics.js`
- Test: `engine/test/physics.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 2 and 3.
- Produces: `World.setHands(list, t)` where `list` is `[{ id, spheres: [{ p: [x, y, z], r }], plate: null | { centre: [x, y, z], normal: [x, y, z], half: [hx, hy], axes: [[x, y, z], [x, y, z]] } }]`; the world derives each sphere's and plate's velocity from the previous call (`p` differences over `t`, clamped to 4 m/s, ignored when not finite) and treats them as kinematic bodies in the contact solver (infinite mass; the cube gets the hand's velocity at the contact point, so a push from off-centre spins it).
- Produces: `World.hold(body, { p, q }, opts = { spring: 20, speed: 5, breakAway: 0.3 })` → `'holding' | 'released'` (a velocity motor towards the target pose, the body stays under the contact solver so walls stop it, returns `'released'` and clears `body.held` when the body is further than `breakAway` from the target); `World.release(body)` (keeps its velocity and angular velocity: a throw).
- Produces: `physics.handSpheres(source, margin = 0.012)` → spheres from a hand source's `points` and `joints` (the shape in `apps/cube/cube.js` `step`), and `physics.plate(grip)` → the plate for a `grab.js` platform grip (`grip.palm`: read `engine/client/grab.js` `PLAT`, `platZ`, `onPlatform` and build the plate from `palm.cx, cy, ang, tilt`).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**: `a fingertip sphere moving at 1 m/s into a resting cube sets it moving at about that speed` (within 20%); `an off-centre push turns it` (angular velocity above 0.5 rad/s); `a held cube follows a moving target and turns with its orientation`; `hold released beyond 0.3 m`; `held through a wall it stays on the near side`; `release keeps the velocity and spin of the hold` (target moves at 2 m/s then release: it flies at about 2 m/s); `a cube on a flat plate is carried along`; `tilting the plate past atan(0.45) slides the cube off`; `a hand teleporting 5 m in one call does not launch the cube above 4 m/s`; `NaN hand input is ignored`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** them. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** the signatures. The hold motor sets `v = clamp(spring * error, speed)` and `w` from the quaternion error (`2 * vector part of target * conj(q)` times `spring`, clamped at 10 rad/s), then lets the solver resolve contacts.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** the file and `node --test`. Expected: PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `git commit -m "physics: hands, hold, throw, flat-hand plate"`.
